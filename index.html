About Computer Engineering Department

Since 2001

The Department of Computer Engineering at PCCOE is committed to value-added quality technical education in Computer Engineering. The strong pillars of our department for undergraduate (B.Tech), postgraduate (M.Tech), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programs are State-of-the-Art Infrastructure, Competent & Research-Oriented Faculty, and Hardworking Students.

In addition to core academics, students are provided with extensive opportunities to develop and excel in curricular, co-curricular, and extra-curricular areas such as collaborative research, skill enhancement workshops, higher studies guidance, industrial internships, and high-tier placements. The program fosters holistic development to mold graduates into ethically sound and globally competent technology leaders.

Modern Infrastructure

High-performance computing clusters, GPU workstations, and dedicated SIG research centers.

Competent Faculty

Distinguished PhD holders, IEEE/ACM mentors, and accomplished researchers with global citations.

Student Triumphs

National hackathon winners, patents granted, and prestigious admits in world-top universities.

M1
Contemporary Curriculum: Develop technologically competent and self-sustained professionals through dynamic, market-aligned academic curriculum.
M2
Innovative Thinking: Nurture innovative thinking and collaborative interdisciplinary research making a direct positive impact on society.
