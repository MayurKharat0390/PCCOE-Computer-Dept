Autonomous Examination & Credit System FAQs

Guidelines
FAQ 1
How does the autonomous grading system work? The department follows a 10-point relative grading scale benchmarked against AICTE and SPPU guidelines with continuous internal assessments (CIE) and end-semester evaluations (ESE).
FAQ 2
Can undergraduate students earn Honors or Minor degrees? Yes, students maintaining a CGPA $\ge 7.5$ can register for specialized Honors/Minors in Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, or Data Science starting from Semester V.
